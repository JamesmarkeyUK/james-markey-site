Our Story

Cooking that tastes of where we're from.

Blue Sardinia opened its doors on Sydenham Road in August 2005, the first restaurant in Guildford to put real Sardinian cooking on the map. Two decades later we're still family-run, still greeting regulars by name, and still cooking the dishes our grandmothers taught us.

Sardinia is home to some of the longest-living people on earth, and their secret sits quietly on our menu: garden vegetables, fava beans, sheep's-milk cheeses, good olive oil and the flavonoid-rich Cannonau wines of the island. It's food that's generous and honest — built for sharing and lingering.

Three things have never changed in twenty years: the ingredients, the welcome and the wine.

Seafood that arrives fresh, vegetables at their peak and herbs grown the island way. We cook to the seasons, so the menu shifts gently through the year.

Island cellar

A hand-picked list of Sardinian and Italian wines — from crisp Vermentino to deep, age-worthy Cannonau — chosen to sit beside every plate.

Treated like family

Hospitality you can feel from the door. Whether it's a quiet lunch for two or a long table of twelve, you're our guest for the evening.
