As someone living (somewhat successfully) with Bipolar Disorder it’s wonderful to get involved in the Bipolar UK photoshoot in preparation for the launch of the new website in the new year.

<ProseFigure src="./images/image-01.jpg" alt="" caption="Team Maximum Effort" />

We were hosted by the psychiatric department in an Oxford hospital and were warmly received. We moved around various rooms to give new backgrounds for the various photos and did both individual and together shoots.

The strangest part is acting so intimate with people you’ve only just met for the first time. As the day went on we all bonded well as a team and the time just flew by.
